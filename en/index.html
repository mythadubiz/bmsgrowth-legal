Privacy — October 27, 2026

This policy applies to BMS Growth (Body Mind & Soul Growth), published by Mythadubiz, the controller for the processing described here. It covers the French and English iOS app.

Your library stays on your device

Your goals, interests, favorites, history and progress are stored on your device. No BMS Growth account is required. Texts and narrations are bundled with the app. We do not request access to your microphone, contacts, GPS location or health data. Any system backups depend on your Apple settings.

Analytics only with your permission

Google Firebase Analytics is disabled by default. Accepting or declining analytics does not change your access. With permission, we measure general use: onboarding steps, opening and completing a reading, offer displays, and the start and result of purchases or restores. We do not send the texts you read, card identifiers, meditation themes, searches, goals or favorites.

Information received by Google

Events may be associated with a pseudonymous installation identifier, language, device model, operating system and app versions, usage dates and durations, approximate location derived from the network connection and in-app purchase events. Firebase technical services may process installation diagnostic information. A pseudonymous identifier is not anonymous data. There are no ads, advertising identifiers or cross-app advertising tracking. Advertising consent settings remain disabled.

Purpose and legal basis

Optional analytics relies on your consent and helps us understand use, improve the app and check its commercial operation. We do not sell this data. The publisher accesses reports in Google Analytics; Google provides the infrastructure. Data may be processed outside your country, including in the United States, under applicable Google terms and contractual safeguards.

Retention and withdrawal

Our Google Analytics property retains detailed event and user data for two months, without resetting retention on each activity. Aggregated reports may remain available longer; this period does not describe all provider technical logs. You can turn off analytics in Settings → Usage analytics, or through Privacy. The app then blocks new events and asks the SDK to disable collection and reset local analytics data. A request already in progress may finish. Withdrawal does not automatically delete data already received by Google or affect the lawfulness of earlier processing.

Apple purchases

Apple handles payments, subscriptions and restores. We do not receive your payment card numbers. The app uses Apple-verified transactions to determine access. If you accept analytics, Firebase may also measure purchase events and product information. Manage or cancel your subscription through Apple subscription settings; declining analytics does not cancel a subscription.

Your rights and support

Use the contact form below for questions or requests for access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection where those rights apply. Describe your request without sending sensitive data. As the app has no named account, we may need additional information to locate relevant data, without requesting unnecessary information. You may complain to your competent data protection authority, including the CNIL in France. Support communications are separate from optional analytics and are used to handle your request.

Children and content

BMS Growth is a general learning and well-being app, not a medical service or an app specifically designed for children. We do not seek to collect personal data from children. Contact us if you believe collection is inappropriate.

This website and changes

This page adds no analytics tool or cookie. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may retain technical logs, including IP addresses, to provide and secure hosting. Links to Apple, Google and the contact form open separate services. Substantial collection changes will be explained in this policy and, where needed, subject to renewed consent.
